About Us

Empowering You to Take Control of Your Financial Future

At Money Skills Academy, we believe that understanding money shouldn’t be complicated or intimidating. Our mission is to make financial education accessible, practical, and easy to understand for people at every stage of life.

Based in Chesterfield, Money Skills Academy provides personalized, one-on-one financial education designed to help you build the knowledge and confidence you need to make informed decisions about your money.

Learning Made Personal

Everyone’s financial situation is different. That’s why our sessions are designed around you.

Through one-on-one appointments, we provide supportive guidance alongside books, resources, and carefully designed materials that walk you through practical financial concepts step by step. Whether you're just starting to learn about money or looking to improve the way you manage your finances, our goal is to give you the tools and understanding to move forward with confidence.

Who We Help

Our services are designed for a wide range of learners, from high-school age through adults in their 40s. You don't need previous financial knowledge or experience to get started.

Whether you're learning the basics for the first time or looking to strengthen your existing financial skills, our approach is built to meet you where you are.

Our Approach

We focus on:

Personalized one-on-one sessions

Simple, practical financial education

Step-by-step learning materials

Building confidence around financial decisions

Accessible education with no previous experience required

We want you to leave each session with a clearer understanding of the topic, practical knowledge you can apply, and greater confidence in managing your financial life.

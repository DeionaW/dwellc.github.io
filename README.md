# dwellc.github.io

What is this site for? This website is for DW Enterprise LLC, a small business that provides books and one-on-one services designed to help young adults improve their financial knowledge and make informed financial decisions.

Who, specifically, is the audience? The primary audience is young adults, beginning with individuals transitioning out of high school and extending through their 40s.

What should a visitor do after landing on your homepage? Visitors should complete a contact form providing information about their financial goals and the areas where they need assistance. This information will help determine the direction of their personalized learning plan.

What does this audience already care about? The audience is primarily interested in building, protecting, and securing their financial future.

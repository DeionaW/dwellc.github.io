# dwellc.github.io

What is this site for? This is the site for DW Enterprise LLC, a small business working that ushers book sales and one-on-one services to assist the young adult community with financial planning and assistance through advisement.

Who, specifically, is the audience? This is specifically geared toward young adults starting with those who are leaving high school up to those in their 30s

What should a visitor do after landing on your homepage? After visiting the homepage a visitor should fill out a contact form with details that will best help figure out which direction the individual requires the most assistance in to create their personal learning plan.

What does this audience already care about? The audience mainly cares about building and securing their financial future.

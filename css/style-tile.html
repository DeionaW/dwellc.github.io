Money Skills Academy

Money Skills Academy is a financial education service designed to help clients build practical money skills and become more confident in managing their financial lives. Located in Chesterfield, the academy offers one-on-one sessions supported by books, resources, and educational materials designed to guide each person through the steps toward becoming more financially empowered.

Our services are designed for individuals ranging from high school age through their 40s. No prior financial experience is required, and each class is designed to meet clients where they are in their financial journey.

Appointments are available online through our intake forms. We accept appointments Monday through Saturday between 7:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m.

Palette

Primary, Forest Green #324A2B. Used for the header, navigation, and primary brand elements. This color represents growth, stability, and financial confidence.

Neutral, Platinum #D1D5D2. Used for primary text and supporting content. The light neutral creates contrast against the Deep Forest background while maintaining a professional appearance.

Accent, Gold #E5950C. Used for section headings, highlights, and important visual elements. Gold represents opportunity, growth, and wealth building.

CTA background, Gold #E5950C. Used for calls to action such as appointment and enrollment buttons. The dark text against the Gold background creates a strong visual distinction for important actions.
